# Technical specification - Revamping of packaging line 3

Client: Pastificio Valsangone S.p.A. Contractor: Automazioni Dora S.r.l. Document sent by the client on 18 November 2026, revision 1.

1. Scope. This specification supplements the signed order and describes the technical requirements for replacing the PLC, the control panel and the operator interface of packaging line 3.

2. Operator interface. The new HMI must be bilingual, Italian and English, with the language switch on the main screen and without restarting the panel. All alarm messages must be translated into both languages.

3. Acceptance tests. The acceptance test takes place at the client's plant with three pasta shapes: penne rigate, fusilli and spaghetti. For each shape the line must run for two consecutive hours at nominal speed, with no stops caused by the automation.

4. Acceptance test date. The client requires the acceptance test to be completed by 15 January 2027, so that the line can return to full production in mid-January.

5. Late penalty. If the acceptance test is completed after the date in point 4, the client applies a penalty of 0.5% of the order value for each working day of delay, up to a maximum of 10%.

6. Motors. For consistency with the spare parts of the other lines, the client asks for motors from its preferred supplier, Motori Canavese S.r.l. Other brands are allowed only with the written approval of the client's purchasing office.

7. Client contact. For technical matters during the revamping and the acceptance test, the client's contact is Mr Venturi, the plant's maintenance manager, who also approves the acceptance test report.

8. Documentation. The operating manual for the operators, in Italian and English, must be delivered to the client at least one week before the acceptance test.
